The app

Do all five. Get your stamp.

The Safari Five app is your checklist in the bush. Tick off the five, stamp your area while you are in a Safari Five area, and send it in. We send back your Completed stamp and put your name in the Hall of the Five.

  1. 1

    Get the app

    Sign up below and we email it to you, or open it now. No app store needed: add it to your home screen and it works in the bush, even with no signal.

  2. 2

    Tick off the five

    Mark each experience as you do it, across one trip or several camps.

  3. 3

    Stamp your area

    While you are in a Safari Five area, tap Stamp my area. Your phone works out which area you are in, with no signal needed. Your location never leaves your phone and is never saved: we only receive the area name.

  4. 4

    Send it in

    Once all five are ticked and stamped, send it. If you are offline, it sends itself when you are back in range. We email your Completed stamp and add your name to the wall.

How to install

Put it on your home screen.

No app store needed. Open the app once on your phone, add it to your home screen, and it works like any other app, even with no signal.

iPhone

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears, or open the menu (⋮).
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

Install it before you leave home, while you have signal. Your ticks and stamp are saved on your phone and send themselves when you are back in range.

Where the stamp counts

Safari Five areas.

Your stamp counts in the main Safari Five areas and in every other park where camps offer walking safaris. Each area is drawn generously around the park and the camps and concessions around it. More will be added.

You are never tracked. The app checks your location on your phone only when you tap Stamp, and only to see which Safari Five area you are in. Your location never leaves your phone and is never saved. We receive only the area name. The Hall of the Five shows only your first name, last initial, the month and year you finished, the country you did it in and your home country.

Safari operators: to ask for your camp's park or safari area to be added, email info@safarifive.com with the camp name, the park or area, and its location (GPS coordinates or a map pin). Areas are added at our discretion.
