Where to find it

Where the Safari Five comes together.

Many places offer one or two of the five. Few offer all five at once. Here is an honest guide to where to look.

The map

Where the classic safari still lives.

Each dot is a park where camps take guests out on foot. The bigger the dot, the more camps there are built around walking. Where walking is strong, the other four are rarely far away.

Popular destinations

Six regions where the five come together.

These are some of the best-known places to do all five. They are not the only ones.

Walking and night drives

Zambia

  • Seasonal bushcamps in the Luangwa, Lower Zambezi and Kafue
  • Daily walks with a guide and armed scout
  • Night drives allowed in the national parks
  • Lion, leopard and wild dog

All five are widely available; pick the camps with care.

Superb walking

Mana Pools and Hwange, Zimbabwe

  • Some of Africa's finest walking, led by Professional Guides
  • Small seasonal camps
  • Lion, leopard and wild dog present
  • Night drives where camps hold private concessions

A walker's paradise; check each camp for night drives.

Big Three country

Northern Botswana

  • Private concessions with night drives
  • Wild dog strongholds
  • Walking in some concessions, not in Moremi

Excellent predators; walking depends on the concession.

Night drives and trails

Greater Kruger, South Africa

  • Night drives at most private-reserve lodges
  • Walking trails and a few walking camps
  • Lion and leopard; wild dog present but scarcer

Choose a smaller camp and a dedicated walking product.

Wild and remote

Ruaha and Nyerere, Tanzania

  • Small seasonal camps and fly camps
  • Walking with an armed ranger
  • Lion, leopard and wild dog

Night drives depend on where the camp sits; ask before you book.

Conservancy safaris

Kenya

  • Small camps on private and community conservancies
  • Walking and night drives on many conservancies
  • Lion and leopard; wild dog in parts of Laikipia
  • Community conservation you can see

Choose a conservancy camp; the national reserves do not allow night drives.

More places to look

Plenty of other wild places offer several of the five.

Mozambique's Gorongosa, Zambia's North Luangwa, Botswana's Linyanti and Selinda, the Serengeti and the Mara, and many more. Each offers several of the five; walking, night drives and the full Big Three vary by park and by camp. Use the five questions below to check any camp you are considering.

Zambia spotlight

South Luangwa, Zambia

The birthplace of the walking safari

More camps here are built around walking than in any other park in Africa. Walking safaris began here in the 1950s, and night drives are allowed in the park. All five come together here as naturally as anywhere on the continent.

24
walking-led camps, the most of any park in Africa
36
camps in all, unfenced and seasonal
About half
of all game activity is on foot (49–62%)

Next most: Mana Pools, Zimbabwe (16 walking-led camps) and Greater Kruger, South Africa (15). Figures from the walkingsafaris.com directory of 201 camps.

How to check any safari for the five

  1. Bushcamp: how many guests does the camp take, and is it fenced?
  2. Walk: are walks offered every day, and who leads them?
  3. Conservation: what does your stay support, and can you see it?
  4. Big Three: are lion, leopard and wild dog all resident nearby?
  5. Night drive: are night drives allowed where you are staying?

For walking in particular, the independent guide walkingsafaris.com lists every walking safari camp in Africa.
