The Big Three carnivores

Lion, leopard and wild dog, in one place.

What it is

The Big Three are Africa's three great apex hunters: lion, leopard and African wild dog. Many parks have one or two of them. Few have all three in numbers that give you a real chance of seeing each in a single stay.

Why it matters

The Big Five is a list of animals to tick off. The Big Three are the pulse of an ecosystem: where all three thrive, the whole system is working. Watching a leopard leave on the hunt or a wild dog pack on the move is the kind of sighting people talk about for years.

Where to find it

The Big Three thrive together in a handful of places: Zambia's Luangwa and Kafue, Zimbabwe's Hwange and Mana Pools, northern Botswana, the Greater Kruger, and Tanzania's Ruaha and Nyerere among them. Africa has only around 6,600 wild dogs left, so they are always the hardest of the three.

What counts

  • All three resident in the area you visit
  • Enough time to look: seven nights or more gives the best odds
  • Guides who know the territories
  • Night drives, for the leopard

Tips

  • Stay longer and cover more ground: move between camps
  • Ask where the wild dog packs have been denning
  • Do not skip the night drive: that is when leopards hunt
