For safari operators

Bring the Safari Five to your guests.

The Safari Five is free for any camp, lodge or safari operator to use. If your guests can do the five with you, tell them about it. It costs nothing and there is nothing to sign.

Using the name

You are welcome to use the words Safari Five to describe the experiences you offer: on your website, in itineraries, brochures and social posts, and on game drives. Please keep it accurate. If you offer all five, say so. If you offer some of them, say which. The badges below make it easy.

In camp

  • Put the room card (A6, with a QR code to the app) in rooms, the mess tent or the bar.
  • Ask guides to mention it on the first drive or walk.
  • Make the stamp a moment: send it in together at the farewell dinner.
  • Share your guests' stamps with #SafariFive and tag @TheSafarifive.

Is your area missing?

Email info@safarifive.com with your camp name, the park or safari area, and its location (GPS coordinates or a map pin). Areas are added at our discretion.

Badges for your website

Three free badges on a simple honor code. Pick the one that fits you best. The more places they appear, the more people discover the Safari Five.

Experience the Five

For camps, lodges and safari operators anywhere in Africa whose guests can experience all five with them:

  1. A true bushcamp that meets the bushcamp criteria.
  2. A walking safari with a qualified walking guide, and an armed scout or ranger where required.
  3. A conservation story you can see, with real visits or meetings.
  4. The Big Three: lion, leopard and wild dog all live in the area.
  5. A night drive, where night drives are allowed.

The honor code

We do not inspect, certify or rank camps. By showing a Safari Five badge, you agree to use it honestly, as described above, and to take it down if that changes. A badge is not an endorsement or a rating by the Safari Five. We may ask anyone to remove a badge if we believe it is not being used honestly, and our decision is final (see our terms).

A few simple rules

  • Be accurate about which of the five you offer.
  • Do not suggest that the Safari Five rates, ranks or endorses your camp. It does not.
  • Use the logos and badges as they are: do not redraw, recolor or combine them with your own logo.
  • Celebrate what you do. Never use the Safari Five to knock another camp or style of safari.
  • For any other use of the logos, the stamp or the Hall of the Five, email info@safarifive.com first.
