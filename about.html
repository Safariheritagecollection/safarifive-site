About

An idea born from a love of safari.

The Safari Five was created by The Safari Heritage Collection, out of a love of safari and its history: the early tented safaris of East Africa, the first bushcamps and walking safaris of southern Africa, and the guides, scouts and communities who have kept wild places wild ever since.

It is run by the Zambia Wildlife & Community Foundation, a charity that supports wildlife protection and the communities who live alongside wildlife. That is fitting: the Safari Five begins with a love of safari, and the people who come to love the bush are the people who come to care for it. Guardians of the Safari Five are those who support that work, through the foundation or any group looking after a wild place they love.

We created it to celebrate what safari has always been about. The Big Five, for all its fame, is a list of sightings. The Safari Five is about the experiences around them: wild country, the guides who read it, the people who keep it wild, and time to take it all in. People who come to love the bush come to care for Africa, and that is the point. The five experiences on this site are the ones that make a safari memorable, and the ones guests describe, year after year, as the reason they come back.

Anyone is welcome to use the name to describe these experiences, and any camp in a Safari Five area can tell its guests about it (see For safari operators). The Safari Five name, logos, stamp and Hall of the Five are trademarks of Safari Heritage Group. You can do all five in many places, and this site will always say so honestly.

For press

The Safari Five, in one line.

"The Safari Five are the five experiences at the heart of the classic African safari: a true bushcamp, a walking safari, a conservation story you can see, the Big Three carnivores (lion, leopard and wild dog) and a night drive. They are the experiences people picture when they dream of Africa, and the ones that call them back."
